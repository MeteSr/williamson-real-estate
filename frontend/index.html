Why Pelican Bay?

Pelican Bay is Daytona Beach’s premier gated community — a master-planned enclave where resort-style living meets strong investment fundamentals.

Gated & Secure

24-hour staffed entry, private streets, and a close-knit neighborhood that takes pride in keeping the community safe.

Golf & Recreation

Adjacent to LPGA International Golf Club, with on-site tennis, pickleball, fitness facilities, and resort-style pools.

Waterfront Living

Serene lakes and canals wind throughout the community, offering scenic views and coveted waterfront home sites.

Prime Location

Minutes from Daytona Beach Shores, I-95, top-rated dining, Halifax Health Medical Center, and world-class motorsports.

Strong Market

Consistent appreciation and limited inventory keep Pelican Bay one of Volusia County’s most in-demand addresses year after year.

Vibrant Community

Year-round social events, an active HOA, and a welcoming mix of families, retirees, and professionals who love where they live.
